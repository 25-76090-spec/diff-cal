How to Play

The dice number sets the question level: 1 = Power Rule, 2 = Chain Rule, 3 = Quotient Rule, 4 = Trigonometric, 5 = Higher-Order, and 6 = Mixed Challenge. Answer correctly to move the number of spaces rolled; an incorrect answer costs one life and you stay put. You play as Player 1 against a computer bot (Player 2), which rolls and answers automatically. Each player starts with 5 lives. If a player loses all 5 lives, the game ends and the other player wins.

Land on a ladder to climb up, or a snake to slide down.

Reach square 100 with an exact roll to win!
